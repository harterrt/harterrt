How it works: The Magnus formula estimates saturation vapor pressure at a given temperature: eₛ(T) = 6.112 × exp(17.67T / (T + 243.5)) where T is in °C. When you heat air without adding moisture, the actual vapor pressure stays at eₛ(T₀) (the saturation pressure at the starting temp). Relative humidity becomes eₛ(T₀) / eₛ(T) × 100%. This is why winter air feels so dry indoors — cold outside air at 100% RH plummets to ~20% when heated to room temperature.
